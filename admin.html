About sign-in and access

Every colleague uses their own Supabase account; Orbit never sees or stores passwords. Admins may authorise, deactivate or remove email addresses. Removed or deactivated accounts immediately lose database access, although any data previously downloaded to their own device cannot be remotely erased. All active users share the same customer records and tasks; administrator rights apply to user management only.
